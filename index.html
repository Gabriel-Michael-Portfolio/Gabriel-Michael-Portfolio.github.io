Short Films

Freelance Work

This is my work for a channel with over 180,000 subscribers. I have made more than these three, but these are my favorites with one reaching almost 350,000 views. The appreciation of my work culminated in the owner of the channel to give me a shot at my own video, see the third thumbnail.
